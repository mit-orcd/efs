# W125 P4 early measurement — background packer on a private cluster — 2026-10-18

**Verdict: packing works end to end; space PASS; foreground impact while
packing concurrently FAILS the 10% target (−12% with 16 workers, −20% with
one writer). Not yet tuned.**
- **Space:** 60,000 tiny files packed into 156 segments: 10.6 MB of segment
  files per node against 4.18 GB of per-file fragments on the base build
  (395× less). Inode-path directories fell from 120,598 to 37 per node.
- **Packing rate:** all 60,000 files packed during the 42 s workload plus
  17 s after it (about 1,000 files/s), with 2 s minimum age.
- **Delete:** a whole packed tree deleted with `rm -rf` retired every segment
  within 20 s, with no copying (2,000-file check).
- **Foreground, packer idle** (candidate index only): −5.4% (16 workers) and
  −3.6% (one writer); one run each, within likely run-to-run noise.
- **Foreground, packer active:** −12.2% and −20.3%; close p99 5.3 → 7.4 ms
  and 1.0 → 3.1 ms.
- **Metadata:** +2,500 Raft entries for 60,000 files (+1%).

## Findings

1. **The design holds:** fast write path unchanged, files packed seconds
   after they go quiet, per-file fragments and inode directories retired
   through the batched journal, whole-tree deletes retire segments without
   copying.
2. **The packer competes with the foreground.** The one-writer case loses
   20%, mostly in close p99. Causes to check: the packer's fragment reads
   and segment PUTs on the same nodes, its Raft proposals (admit, switch,
   unindex, GC acks) on the busiest group, and the superseded-fragment
   journal deletions.
3. **Candidate index cost** (PI) is within noise in one run; repeat runs are
   needed before attributing the 4–5%.

## Next (P4 continues)

- A rate budget and back-off on foreground latency, then re-measure; the
  target is at most 10% while packing and noise when idle.
- Repeat each configuration three times.
- Compaction of low-live segments; backfill of existing small files.
- Read-back verification once P3 lands.

## Reproduce

On node9901, with a base build in `BASE` and a packer build in `PACK`
(each with `efsd`, `efs-fuse`, `efs-mgmt`, `scripts/client.sh`):

```sh
scripts/w125_p4_measure.sh BASE PACK OUT 17680
```

Prerequisites: free ports 17681–17683, `mlx5_0`, the private libfuse, about
15 GB free under `/tmp`. Runtime about 6 minutes. The script removes its run
