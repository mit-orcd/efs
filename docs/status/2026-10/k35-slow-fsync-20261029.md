## Problem and fix

The [AF gate and untouched-base reproduction](af-status-snapshot-20261028.md)
both fail B6 slow mode: F0 fsync EIO at 60.07/60.02 s, while the other two
fragment holders have confirmed durable data. The 65 s COMMIT fault outlasts
the existing 60 s caller budget. `stripe_prove` joined every COMMIT worker before
attempting the required durable degraded mark, so no time remained for proof.
The failure is availability, not permission to acknowledge unproven writes.

Candidate `1ab9fef83` changes only `src/client/data_commit.c` and its existing
production-code component test. Each worker keeps its original absolute
COMMIT deadline. A bounded completion group lets the coordinator wait until
all COMMITs finish, or until the last quarter of the remaining budget
(capped at 5 s). At that point it folds **only completed** replies and can write
a required degraded mark using two matching durable fragments. All workers
continue with their original deadlines and are joined before fsync returns.
The final pass can use additional completed durability evidence; it does not
repeat a failed proof RPC. No detached worker or checked-out connection can
outlive its stack job. Worker results are published under the group mutex.

The overall 60 s bound and any shorter inherited deadline are never reset or
extended. Metadata keeps that same deadline. A mark received after expiry,
one durable fragment, an uncertain/lost incarnation or a failed mark without
three durable fragments still fails closed and retains obligations. Terminal
closure plus fresh authoritative absence checks remain unchanged. No new
setting, protocol field, retry policy or durability exception. B6's 65 s fault,
fsync/read bounds and every payload/protection/restart assertion are unchanged.
The change fixes proof ordering; it does not promise to return before the
slow COMMIT's existing timeout and cleanup.

## Regression and negative controls

slow-proof cases use a shorter 1 s caller budget to make the boundary practical:

- two matching durable fragments plus a withheld third obtain a mark before
  the unchanged deadline; captured COMMIT deadlines equal the caller's;
- a failed mark is attempted once, returns EIO and leaves obligations;
- one durable fragment remains insufficient;
- all three COMMITs can finish after the early-proof point but before the
  original deadline, succeeding without a mark (no storage deadline cut);
