# D4 reclaim lag: abandoned uploads from refused unconditional images (Oct 26)

**Purpose.** Open 1 of the [D4 drain page](d4-pack-drain-20261025.md), for
release check D4 (bounded progress with packing, indexing and KV
maintenance active) in the [alpha 1.0 checklist](alpha-1.0-2plus1-checklist.md).
Under the fixed-working-set rewrite load, quota use grew linearly all run.
intel1 reached 52 % of 8 GiB at 60 minutes; F1's xorinox runs reached 31 %;
amd hit ENOSPC at about 45 minutes. Yet the stores held 13–64 MB after
the run.

**Verdict.** Branch `claude/interesting-mclean-65362a`. Fix `66bfc2786`,
driver checks `66bfc2786`, `525de5b74` and `76381b5bd`, test fix
`d5f492d31`.

## How the cause was found

1. **Physical, not accounting.** On a node, `used` equalled the bytes
   written minus the bytes GC removed: 3.2 GB written, 2.66 GB removed,
   541 MiB used at the end of an 8-minute load. The GC record queue stayed
   short. The frag pass found about 180 records in its prefix each time.
   Its pending estimate drifts upward by design (D26), so it read
   thousands. Packed segments held about 24 MiB.
2. **The orphan ledger.** `efs-mgmt status` sampled every 30 s during a
   5-minute load: `Abandoned uploads: pending=198 → 3348`, `collected=0
   fragments_deleted=0`. D31 deletes an abandoned body only after the
   client's session completed a global revocation past the ticket's
   epoch. The client sets its epoch once (`opid_epoch = 1`) and revokes it
   at clean unmount. So a live mount's abandoned uploads stay until it
   unmounts. That is why the stores were small after each run.
3. **Which rewrites abandon.** Loopback probes (`probes/` in the raw
   evidence): rewrites with lengths that are multiples of the 128 KiB
   chunk abandoned almost nothing (6 of 1200 operations; 0–3 with 8
   concurrent writers, D4 packer settings or the 256 KiB memtable). The
   D4 driver's exact operations, with a new unaligned 192–512 KiB length
   each time, abandoned about two uploads per operation: overwrite +360,
   truncate +423, in-place +399 per 200.
4. **Why.** A diagnostic build logged each rejecting settle and the STALE
   reason of each transaction PREPARE (`diag/` in the raw evidence). For
   six resizing rewrites of one file, every rewrite rejected all chunks of
   its first upload. Every PREPARE STALE was `why=6`
   (`EFS_PUB_STALE_FOLD_LIST`) on the previous write's tail chunk, a full
   image (`dlen=0`). The client trace showed that chunk sent with base
   `ffffffffffffffff` (`EFS_CHUNK_BASE_UNCOND`). The previous write had
   published its partial tail as a span. The new full image carried the
   client's span list, empty after its truncate, while the row still
   listed that span. The fold check ("the merged image covers exactly the
   deltas the writer read") refused it. That failed the unit and with it
   every chunk (`report-stale: ... committed=0 replayed=N`). The replay
   uploaded each chunk again, and the first uploads were settled REJECTED.

`host_pub_pack_inner` already resolved an unconditional image's base
against the committed row (`expected_gen = got.generation`). An aligned,
last-writer-wins image contains none of the old bytes, so the span list
it replaces is likewise the committed one. The fix reads the row's span
count and newest seq (`efs_meta_apply_get_chunk_deltas`) for such a
record. The apply's fold check then passes, and the apply queues the
replaced spans' GC as for any full image.

## Open

1. **Residual abandoned uploads (D4/client).** About 3.5 % of `O_TRUNC`
   overwrites still abandon an upload (7 in 200 in the probe; 610 in the
   60-minute run). Each waits for unmount: about 27 MiB per node per hour
   at this load, so a mount that lives for weeks still grows. Two ways to
   close it: find and remove that path, or let a live mount rotate its
   session epoch through the D31 barrier so its abandoned uploads become
   collectable. Rotation also revokes leases and locks held under the old
   epoch, so it is a protocol change. Owner: D4/client, D31.
2. **The packer packs files that are being rewritten in place.** In
   `test_pack_dup_candidate.py` files rewritten every 2 s packed early,
   also at a 10 s minimum age (28–39 packs for 24 files). The candidate's
   mtime probably does not see the lane mtime marks of in-place writes.
   Costs repacks; not attributed.
3. **GC pending estimate drift.** `efs-mgmt gc-status` pending_estimate
   reached 459 293 in the hour while the real queue stayed in the
   hundreds. D26 allows upward drift (it costs one extra bounded scan).
   As an operator signal it misleads.
