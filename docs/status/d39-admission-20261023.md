**Stage 1 — configuration, identity, W153a, store, boot counter (complete).**

- `cluster.conf` (C1): `src/common/cluster_conf.c`, `include/efs/cluster_conf.h`.
  The parser agrees with the model's `parse_config` on all 28 test inputs
  (accept or refuse, `tests/test_d39_admission.py`); the digest is
  SHA-256 of `efs-cluster-config 1\n` plus `"<id> <ipv4>:<port>\n"` per member
  in id order and equals the model's digest. `efsd --cluster FILE`; the
  record whose endpoint equals `--addr:--port` is the member (a
  non-canonical address never matches). `--node-id`, `--join`,
  `--no-persist`, the persisted node table (`meta/cluster_nodes.bin`),
  rejoin and HELLO gossip are removed; the node table is the configured
  list and HELLO only refreshes a configured member's own storage facts.
  `EFS_MD_RAFT_N` is removed and a set value stops `efsd`
  (W153a), as do a malformed list and an advertised endpoint that is no
  member record, all before anything is created or listened on. The
  metadata member count and group voters come from the list.
- Store ownership and boot counter (C2): `src/server/member_store.c`. An
  exclusive `flock` on `<mdraft>/owner.lock` is taken before the store is
  touched; a second process stops ("owned"). `boot.bin` is 32 bytes (magic,
  version, counter, SHA-256-derived check); it is created at store
  initialization with counter 0, and every start writes
  `max(fresh, saved + 1)` (temp file, fsync, rename, directory fsync)
  before the daemon listens. Missing, short, corrupt, wrong-version and
  exhausted (`2^64−1`) counters stop startup and leave the file unchanged; a
  persistence failure stops startup. `identity.bin` marks initialization
  and binds the store to its member id and configuration digest (another
  id or digest is refused); an uninitialized store with `boot.bin`
  (C13 3) and a store holding Raft/KV data without an identity are
  refused. The incarnation is the Raft `boot_id`.

**Stage 2 — declarations, roles, states, `complete`, FORMATION_READ,
MKFS/SALT pair (complete).**

- Protocol 6 (C4/C5): `CLIENT_HELLO` carries a role (client, mgmt, peer)
  and the identity tuple (member id, endpoint, digest, pair salt or
  unknown, incarnation, counter); the reply carries the responder's tuple,
  its channel and `complete`, also on a refusal. One declaration per
  connection. Peers declare and validate the reply in `peer_connect`
  before the RDMA upgrade (`adm_check_reply`: intended member, configured
  endpoint, then the C5 predicate); only configured members are dialed.
  efs-mgmt declares mgmt with `--cluster FILE` or `EFS_CLUSTER_CONF`
  (not `EFS_CLUSTER`, which the devops and cluster tooling use for the
  cluster name), otherwise client.
- Role allowlists (`efs_role_allow`, C4): peer control (Raft, HELLO,
  status class, FORMATION_READ), peer application (client protocol,
  forwards, segment/GC/fence/rebuild data plane) only on a formed channel
  with `complete`; mgmt in any state (RAFT_MKFS empty, FORMATION_READ,
  status class, RAFT_STEP_DOWN), the client protocol once complete;
  clients only when the admitter is complete (`EFS_ERR_NOT_FORMED`
  otherwise). JOIN and unknown types match no role. Maintenance commands
  (pack-compact, raft-resync, shrink-quota, add-storage; C12 unresolved)
  are served to client and mgmt once complete. Online membership change
  (CFG / `raft-change`, `add-node`) is refused and removed.
- States and `complete` (C3): unformed, forming, formed persisted in
  `identity.bin`; `complete` = `known[0]` and `known[2]` known and equal;
  first `complete` persists formed. A restart re-derives hosted groups from
  their applied records (a conflict with the persisted pair refuses start)
  and non-hosted groups by FORMATION_READ.
- Formation (C10): MKFS is `[now][salt][digest]`, SALT
  `[anchor][salt][digest]`; apply is compare-and-install (first pair wins,
  a different pair is a no-op, a conflict with the local pair stops the
  daemon and halts the group without writing); an install or matching
  repeat is an identity event (counter + 1, outgoing connections retired).
  `raft-mkfs` (mgmt only) answers the committed pair if group 0 has one,
  NOT_PRIMARY with a hint off the group-0 leader, BUSY naming absent
  members (no binding or no validating outgoing connection), otherwise
  proposes MKFS locally. A formation driver thread proposes SALT from the
  group-2 leader once the MKFS pair is known and group 2 has none, and reads
  non-hosted groups' records with FORMATION_READ (answered by the group
  leader after a ReadIndex). `efs-mgmt raft-mkfs` follows hints, retries
  while members are absent (`--wait`, default 120 s) and returns once group
  2 holds the same pair; `efs-mgmt formation-read 0|2`.

**Stage 3 — binding, high-water mark, check-and-register (PARTIAL).**

- Landed: per-member binding = high-water mark (C5 steps 1–4, publication
  under the binding leaf lock, `adm_mu`), C11 fence word (atomic store after
  the binding fields, a pre-filter that only refuses), identity events
  under a separate identity mutex with persistence outside the leaf lock.
  C7 (a) and (b) are evaluated at receive on every declared frame
  (handler gate), again per item at the Raft inbox drain (each item
  carries its connection's declared tuple) and at queued read-proof
  inspection; a Raft frame must name the declared sender and the local
  receiver and carry the declared incarnation. Pooled and Raft-sender
  connections declared before an identity event are retired. Forwarding
  (application work) is not attempted before the local member is complete.
- INCOMPLETE: explicit effect records (registration naming an item's
  effects); C7 (c) owned continuations (a reply is accepted on the
  connection that sent the request, which was validated at declaration,
  without re-checking the responder's binding or pair at consumption);
  snapshot staging checks; the fence control item to the Raft pump (the
  pump's `peer_boot` cache catches up from frames, which the model allows).

**Stage 4 — operation lifecycle (INCOMPLETE, nothing landed).** Tokens
with immutable admitting tuples, phases and acceptance boundaries,
timeout/IO-error outcomes, handoff to obligations, the `raft_entry` /
`receipt` / `fragment` resolvers, the daemon-wide and per-member bounds
(BUSY at the bound), the retry takeover of a held obligation's
reservation, C9 snapshot acceptance rules (the included-term end-to-end
check) and C12 retry authority are not implemented.

Other gaps: readiness is not reported as the three facts of C10 (formation
state is visible in logs and `formation-read`, not in `efs-mgmt status`);
the C13 unresolved points keep the model's choices (SALT from the group-2
leader; uninitialized store with a counter refused).

## Driver and harness changes in this repository

Every committed starter of `efsd` now writes a `cluster.conf` with all
members and passes `--cluster` (no `--node-id`, `--join` or
`EFS_MD_RAFT_N`), and points efs-mgmt at it with `EFS_CLUSTER_CONF` where
it formats or polls before formation: `scripts/server.sh` (third argument
or `EFS_CLUSTER_CONF`), the `scripts/` gate and A/B drivers,
`tests/faults/harness.py`, `tests/live/` and `tests/measure/` drivers,
`tests/valgrind_leaks.sh` (the valgrind'd member now starts first: mkfs
needs every member), `tests/rdma_first_inode.sh`,
`tests/stress/raft_host_smoke.sh`, `tests/clean_cluster.sh`,
`tests/roll_efsd.sh`. Drivers that formed with two members and added a
third later (`k22_follower_bound.sh`, `k22_stalled_follower.sh`,
`w23_stalled_compactor.sh`) now start all members, format, then restart the
third. `--configuration` (raft_read_freshness) and `--four-voters` (w66)
are refused: they need online membership change. Single-daemon wire tests
(`fragment_integrity`, `fragment_tail_smoke`, `io_stats_smoke`) run their
daemon as member 1 of a formed three-member list (`tests/live/d39_cluster.py`).
`scripts/w127_incompatible_entry.sh` probes the halted group with a CREATE
(a repeated raft-mkfs now only reads the committed pair). Process
identification by `--node-id` uses `--port`.
