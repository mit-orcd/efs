# K106: Request in flight to a halted member waits the 30 s transport timeout

- State: OPEN
- Blocks alpha: yes
- Kind: availability with one member unresponsive

## Summary

- Cause: Batch R (devel `60c9fcdad`) FAIL 6 of 6: fsync and close fail after 8 s or 16 s at the halt (one log: the PUT window outlasts its 8 s waiter; RDMA: a REPORT waits out its budget), MKDIR waits 30 s (K114).
- Owners: `src/client/node_cache.c` (`efs_client_conn_await_reply`), `src/client/inode_rpc.c` (`rpc_reply_wait_cut`), `EFS_NODE_PROBE_MS`
- Reproducer: `scripts/evidence/task-gd/k106_halt.py` on fcstor010 to 014 (fails 6 of 6 on devel `60c9fcdad`); `tests/test_node_waiter_cut.py`; live on intel1: first `mkdir` after SIGSTOP, 30 s before, 6.1 s after.
- Invariant: While one member hangs, which is inside the one-member-down promise, no operation in flight waits the 30 s transport timeout or fails.
- Owed: Diagnose and fix the fsync and close errors at the halt (in-flight PUT against `put_win_wait` 8,000 ms; REPORT in flight on RDMA), no raised bound; rerun `k106_halt.py` after it and after the K114 fixes.

## Item

Task FF (Nov 10, seen while fixing K104): three waits a halted member (SIGSTOP or a hung host: the kernel accepts TCP, nothing replies) still causes after the K104 routing fixes. (a) A request already in flight to that member when it stops waits the 30 s transport timeout (`EFS_IO_TIMEOUT_MS`), which is also the client's operation budget, so a mutation can still end in `EBUSY`; a LOOKUP has no operation deadline and waited 36 s. (b) Every PUT placed on the halted member reconnects and pays two 5 s protocol-declaration waits (each churn write took 10 s), because the client's background reprobe (`node_probe_run`) only connects TCP, which a stopped process's kernel accepts, and so clears the down mark. (c) GC recovery of a stranded transaction runs behind the reap step's member acceptance on the GC thread (29 to 30 s). Likely the same waits, seen as failed writes (Nov 10, Task EY, batch D run H15 on candidate `9bb50fe79`, branch `claude/kind-darwin-bbba2f` at `9864e90a6`, not merged): with node 3 stopped by SIGSTOP and before any quota pressure, ten `close()` calls of two 4 MiB writers returned `EIO` in 5,352 s; each matches a client `EFS_ERR_BUSY` from `efs_dcache_flush_ino` (`src/client/write.c`), seven at `stage=put-window` (another flusher's PUT window still open after `put_win_wait(ino, 8000)`), two at `stage=put`, one at `stage=report`. The coordinator's reading, not diagnosed: a PUT that pays wait (b), 10 s, outlasts the 8 s window wait. A fix for (b) must be checked against this symptom
