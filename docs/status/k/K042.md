# K42: Clean unmount fails its session revoke with BUSY (append-reap-owed)

- State: VERIFY
- Blocks alpha: yes
- Kind: clean client stop (E2), alpha blocker

## Summary

- Cause: The revoke's append step waited for the reaper on a dead inode; it now retires the session's rows itself (wave 73), but the 4-member RDMA clean-stop gate failed once before the fix.
- Owners: `efs_meta_retire_append_session`
- Reproducer: `tests/test_k42_reap_owed.py` (named on the fix page): private 4-member TCP cluster, reaper stopped, 20 of 20 clean stops failed before and pass after.
- Invariant: A clean stop on a healthy cluster succeeds and a refusal is certified or fails closed with its reason.
- Owed: Only the rerun on the frozen candidate. Batch R (devel `60c9fcdad`): the 4-member RDMA gate passed 3 of 3, 24 of 24 unmount revokes `rc=0`, longest 539 ms.

## Item

**W164** a clean unmount on a healthy cluster failed its session revoke with BUSY in the cleanup phase and exited 1 (Task A candidate D: peer client on loopback-5 after the 68/68 two-client suite, `revoke rc=-13 phase=cleanup shard=1677 touched=89`, no outstanding tickets). Reproduced on amd at devel `1fc526f66` on the first loaded run: the peer's append cleanup selected its receipt on a shared inode, and `efs_meta_retire_append_session` refused because another session's append was in flight (OPEN reservation). Nothing was proposed, but the server answered a bare BUSY and the client never repeats an uncertified BUSY. The first gate found the lease-phase twin: the cleanup batch applied as a no-op BUSY while the other client's close transaction held the inode's last-close marker, and the reply carried the entry's index

## Disposition
