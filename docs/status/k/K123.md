# K123: TSan lane: packer moves none of the refused files after a member returns

- State: FIXED
- Blocks alpha: no
- Kind: test lane / packer after a member returns (TSan only)

## Summary

- Cause: Two races in the test, no product defect: step 5 took its packed-files counter after node 3 was back (the packer had already moved the files); step 3 read the status once before the last chunk was rebuilt.
- Owners: `tests/test_k37_segment_rebuild.py`
- Reproducer: The TSan entry alone on amd, three loops in parallel: 8 of 52 runs failed before the fix (all the first signature), 60 of 60 pass after.
- Invariant: After a member returns, the packer moves the files it had refused within the bound of the test, also under TSan.
- Owed: Nothing. The status signature was never reproduced (0 of 120 runs); its cause rests on the status text of the wave 80 lane.

## Disposition

Status signature: step 3 waited only for the degraded-segment count and read the status once; it now waits for `Protection: full` and `Cluster state: OK` with the 60 s bound the test already had. No bound raised, no sleep added, the packer unchanged. FIXED and not VERIFIED because the second signature was not reproduced. Was: OPEN, blocks alpha: no (coordinator's ruling at the wave 83 merge: seen only under TSan, as a test assertion and without a sanitizer report, and the same test passes in every ordinary gate; it gets a row because it has failed two of the last four merge lanes with no owner; the user may overrule): not diagnosed (raw on amd, `~maik/efs-artifacts/merge-wave83-20261111-amd-sanitizers/`, `SHA256SUMS` hash `b4613a4842159685`, and `merge-wave80-20261111-amd-sanitizers/`, `6fd7660760694612`; [ledger](../2026-11/ledger.md))
