## Item

Wave 72 merge (Nov 10, amd, TSan lane): `tests/test_k96_status_slots_live.py` failed, 15 of 24 copies with an unclean unmount (`revoke rc=-6` after 15 s), because one member stopped answering. Run alone under TSan, back to back, it failed 3 of 3 on devel `6d54565ec` and 3 of 3 on the wave 72 tree, so the wave did not bring it. A thread dump of the silent member was taken in each of the 6 runs: 20 to 66 threads retry in `host_view_get` (`src/server/raft_host.c`, the lock-free copy of a group's view) while the publish sequence is odd, and one thread is in `host_publish_view` at the second sequence increment, waiting inside the TSan runtime for the lock on that word, which every retrying reader takes in shared mode; the pump and connection threads wait for the host mutex. The reader loop has no bound and no back-off. Not known: how long a reader can spin outside TSan when the publisher is preempted inside its window of 15 atomic stores (not measured), why the wave 71 lane passed this test on amd (the host was rebooted in between), and whether the entry still passes under TSan on fstor007, the host whose lane counted for batch F (last run there on `9bb50fe79`); if it fails there, the next batch F cannot pass its TSan lane
