# K112: One of three RDMA stop loops refused 20 of 20 clean stops (20 s budget)

- State: VERIFY
- Blocks alpha: yes
- Kind: clean stop on a healthy cluster / transaction retry

## Summary

- Cause: Fixed in wave 79 (cause narrowed, the refusal itself not reproduced): while another session held an OPEN append the stop's retirement selected no row; it now retires its completed rows below the floor.
- Owners: `src/meta/append_plan.c` (`efs_meta_retire_append_session`, `floor_row`, `retire_below_floor`)
- Reproducer: `tests/test_append_cleanup.c` (floor cases, fail on devel) and step 1 of `tests/test_k42_peer_stop_open.py`; live: `tests/live/e2_peer_stop_loop.py` on amd, 120 of 120 stops pass.
- Invariant: A clean stop on a healthy cluster must succeed; while an append is open only the stop's own retirement may remove the revoked session's completed rows wholly at or below min(file size, append frontier).
- Owed: Only the candidate rerun: batch R passed 60 of 60 RDMA stops (longest revoke 245 ms). Revokes of 3.5 to 4.7 s under a live writer remain, not addressed.

## Item

Task EY batch D (Nov 10, candidate `9bb50fe79`, `tests/live/e2_peer_stop_loop.py` with five members on one host over RDMA, three loops of 20 clean stops): loops 1 and 2 passed 20 of 20, loop 3 failed 20 of 20. Every stop was refused for the whole 20 s cleanup budget (`rc=-13`, `cleanup_ms` 20,063 to 20,102, `phase=cleanup`), all on `shard=3751`: 16 with `reason=uncertified-busy`, 4 with `reason=append-open-by-other-session`. From 2 s after formation the members logged 2,585 `txn-finish` lines with `abort decided` and `(coordinator lost its group)`, about every 10 ms, 1,329 for coordinator 3751 and 1,256 for coordinator 1, with no leader change; loop 2 had 579 such lines and passed, and one passing cell run had 221, so the lines alone do not cause a refusal

## Disposition

On amd (TCP, one host) 120 of 120 clean stops pass on devel and on the fix; replies naming an OPEN append of another session fell from 86 to 103 per slow loop to 0, and the longest revoke from 6.4 to 7.4 s to 3.5 to 4.7 s. The refusal seen in batch D (20 of 20 stops over RDMA) was never reproduced, so the fix is shown to remove the wait, not that refusal.
