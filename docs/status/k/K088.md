## Item

Task CJ: `tests/test_bench_published.py` fails at 0.57 % of runs at 24 concurrent copies (11 of 1920 on intel2, load average 25 to 38; 0.08 % at 12 and at 8 copies) with a cold-read content mismatch at the correct length. The writer `efs-bench` prints `BENCH_OK` and exits 0 although the client logged `report-stuck ... not re-sent, writeback error` for one chunk. A background async REPORT (`meta_flush_main`) commits a half-filled chunk; the writer's final sync REPORT carries `base=0`, answers STALE, the replay rebuilds an identical record, and `report_rec_stuck` drops it after two rounds (`write.c`). The `rc=-4` in these logs is the test's expected name-collision control (EXIST), and `mkdir ... rc=-13 stage=3` is a transient read-index BUSY (400 ms bound) that the client retries; neither is the cause. `efs-bench` ignores the recorded writeback error, so the loss is silent there (FUSE would return EIO at fsync/close)
