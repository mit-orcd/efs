# K39: write and fsync return EIO with one member down on a 4-member cluster

- State: VERIFY
- Blocks alpha: unstated
- Kind: data path / availability with one member down, alpha blocker

## Summary

- Cause: Fixed (scoped): the put-ticket ADMIT lost its exchange with a stopping leader and returned NET at once; it now re-picks a voter and placement probes a returned member.
- Owners: `rpc_put_ticket`, `put_fragments_parallel_once`
- Reproducer: `tests/test_k35_fsync_degraded.py` (cases 7 and 8) at the 10 s away-mark default.
- Invariant: write and fsync succeed with one member down at the product default away-mark window.
- Owed: Live multi-host stop, RDMA and orcd-mini checks; the K33 payload read EIO stays a separate item.

## Item

`write()`/`fsync` return `EIO` with one member down on a 4-member loopback cluster, 2 of 3 runs of `tests/test_k35_fsync_degraded.py` (cases 7 and 8) once the client's away-mark window is the product default of 10 s (the test's `EFS_PUT_AWAY_MARK_MS=600000`, later the fault-only `EFS_FAULT_AWAY_MARK_MS`, had hidden it; efs-fuse has no fault build, so the setting was a no-op since). Found by the merge gate of W159, the flake sweep, the dead-code sweep and the docs audit; logs kept on intel2 `~/efs-artifacts/merge-wave8b-20261025/k35-keep/`
