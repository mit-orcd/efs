# K88 hint per chunk

[K88 hint per chunk](../k88-hint-per-chunk-20261105.md) (Task DK): **PASS, measured Nov 5**; K88 stays FIXED, blocks alpha: no. The K88 CAS hint table had one slot per inode (`cbase_slot` shifted the chunk index out). When one REPORT committed half images of two chunks of a file, only the last noted chunk kept its committed base, and the other still fell to `report-stuck`. The slot is now (inode, chunk) in the same 4096-entry, 128 KiB table, and DE's per-hint retirement is kept (`13ebe3710`). New fast test `tests/test_k88_chunk_hints_live.py` (library writer, production `efsd`): old slot 3 of 3 files `report-stuck` on chunk 0, new 0 of 3; `test_stale_recovery` aborts on the old slot and passes on the new. How far K88 reached: sequential `efs-bench` (CK's measured shape) was never in the gap: 0 `report-stuck` in 2400 runs before and 2400 after (node9900, 8 copies × 4 writers × 2 MiB). Untraced follow-up: a cluster-wide BUSY stall, about 1 round in 12, under 32 library writers. TSan PASS; budget row 10 s.
