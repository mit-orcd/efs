# K109: a clean unmount past a stalled storage member (Task FO)

[K109: a clean unmount past a stalled storage member (Task FO)](../k109-stalled-member-unmount-20261110.md): **PASS on xorinox (three private members, loopback); the K109 row and the multi-host check are not yet ruled** (Nov 10, branch `claude/sad-brahmagupta-5d44a8` on devel `cff8eb424` with devel `bbe93d148` merged in, code commits `935c39e36`, after the review of `889ea7e7d` `02618815e`, and after the review of `3a984be56` `a42a5cb85`; not merged). `host_storage_fence_members` returned the first error reply of a reachable member, so one member that was alive with its apply stalled (`rc=-15`, or a `SIGSTOP`ped one that never answers, `rc=-6` at the 15 s bound) failed every clean unmount although a strict majority sealed. The fence now asks every member at once, counts only rc OK as sealed, counts an unreached member and one that answered it did not seal alike, keeps STALE, INVAL and PROTO as refusals, and returns when a majority sealed without waiting for the rest (no timeout, sleep or bound changed). Its workers ask over their own connections, at most 32 waiting exchanges per member (past that the member counts unreached at once), and the daemon stop wakes and joins them, from the start of the handler drain: on the first version a stop with a worker parked on a member that never answers failed under ASan (heap-use-after-free on the pooled connection `server_peer_pool_shutdown` had freed), on this one it passes; a stop with an unmount handler waiting on two silent members takes 0.2 s (30.4 s on devel, 30.5 s without the wake). On a healthy cluster the whole revoke is 44 ms at the median against 62 ms on devel (loopback). The live test is budgeted at 200 s (three 8-job gates: 156.3, 165.5, 152.2 s). 20 of 20 unmounts with one stalled member failed after 15.2 to 15.3 s before and exit 0 in 0.3 s after (revoke 35 to 53 ms); `SIGSTOP`ped member 3 of 3 failed and 5 of 5 pass; leaders split with the client on n2 5 of 5 pass; two stalled members still fail closed. The counting table fails on devel for the did-not-seal rows; the live test fails on devel and passes. The page also settles that the revoke needs a strict majority (K54, D46 item 6), not every member, and corrects the guide, `operations.md` and release-note L29. **Owed:** the multi-host check (Task EZ's S2 method with clean unmounts during the stall); an old-epoch PUT against the stalled member after a revoke was not run.
