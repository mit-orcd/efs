## Correction to earlier budget evidence

Previous continuation sparse-pressure records requested 32 MiB hard + 8 MiB
drain, but the allocator rejects a drain below its 32 MiB slab minimum and
uses the 64 MiB default. Those old runs therefore had a **32 + 64 MiB** effective
bound, not 32 + 8. They still establish their recorded bytes/RSS/GC results;
they do not validate the advertised smaller reserve. New bounded gates request
32 + 32 MiB and require the allocator's opt-in effective-budget trace to agree.
No production budget/minimum is changed by this correction.
