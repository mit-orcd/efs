Root cause (src/server/raft_host.c host_sender, src/common/protocol.c)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A frame larger than the RDMA buffer (72 KiB) goes over the conn's TCP
side-channel (conn_pick_send_chan) and the peer answers it on TCP;
smaller frames go RDMA and are answered on RDMA. host_sender pipelines up
to three messages (host_sender_depth = nrecv−1) and then collected three
RAFT_REPLYs with `recv_chan` left at the LAST message's channel. A batch
of [AE_REP (RDMA), big AE (TCP)] read TCP only; the RDMA reply stayed in
the ring; the read timed out; the conn was destroyed; Raft resent the
same window; same shape, same timeout. Only the lane between the two
dual-group hosts mixes one group's AppendEntries with the other group's
replies (the leader keeps one AE in flight per peer, so a single-group
lane rarely batches two kinds), and only a big-entry stream makes the AE
take TCP: the publish batches of a 100 GiB dd (2048 publishes per
command) did. Before F1 the same shape waited EFS_IO_TIMEOUT_MS (30 s)
per attempt — that is the "lost RAFT_REPLY" behind the election storms in
results/measure/20260930-040600-postfix-review; F1 made it a 250 ms
livelock instead.

Consequences, in order: the follower each broken lane fed fell ~700
entries behind; every read it served (`host_read_index` → 400 ms
`apply-sleep` → BUSY) and every REPORT touching both groups returned
BUSY; the client's 16-retry budget (10.3 s) ran out → EBUSY / ENOENT;
dd's close REPORT lost 18 s of pack+push to one BUSY at the end and was
retried whole; the unmount gave up after 60 s. The KV L0 gate
(efs_kv_lsm_l0_hot) was not involved: the KV dir on fcstor004 is 0.33 GB.

Fix (85f5b31c): `efs_conn.last_recv_chan` is set on every receive path;
host_sender counts expected replies per channel after each send and
reads with recv_chan=RDMA while RDMA replies are outstanding (that wait
drains the ring first and falls through to TCP when a byte is there),
TCP otherwise; a reply on a channel with none outstanding is a protocol
error (conn dropped). No wire change.
