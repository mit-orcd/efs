Call graph (both):     host_pump -> apply_committed -> host_apply ->
                       efs_txn_resolve -> lsm_scan_prefix -> merge_scan

Root cause: efs_txn_resolve() and efs_txn_drop() scanned the WHOLE shard
(2-byte prefix) to find the txn's INTENT/GUARD/REDUCE records. On the mount
root's shard (1) that walks every inode/dentry/lane/chunk key and every
uncompacted tombstone through the full L0+L1 segment merge, under the KV
lock and h->mu, on every replica of the group, once per participant shard.
A same-group child takes the log path (no RESOLVE) — 2 ms; a cross-group
child runs the txn — ~1 s. That is the bimodal root mkdir.

Fix (src/meta/txn.c, txn_scan_kinds): scan the three 3-byte prefixes
[shard][INTENT], [shard][GUARD], [shard][REDUCE] instead. Same records
visited (the callbacks already ignored every other kind); no protocol or
on-disk change.
