-------------------------------------

1. HOT PATH: `dcache_reclaim_main` = 39.8 % of efs-fuse cycles.
   16 reclaim threads pop only `have_base` dirty slots (W18). Under
   ecopy the dirty set is fresh files (no base) and dirty_bytes sits
   above the 2 GiB limit, so every kick re-walked all 64 dirty lists
   and found nothing, in a loop. FIX (src/client/write.c): a sweep that
   pops nothing parks the threads until the next kick after a 10 ms
   nap (`kicks`/`empty_kicks`/`empty_until_ns`); a sweep that pops
   re-arms at once.

2. ISSUE: 244 `inode-rpc: slow-ok ... attempts=1 saw_busy=0` with a
   mode at exactly 5.0 s (178), then 10 s (10), max 15.7 s, on every
   RPC type (43/45/47/63/67/83/87). Not the network: the client conn
   pool (`efs_client_conn_get`, node_cache.c) has 16 slots per node,
   a checkout holds a slot for the whole RPC and a PUT holds three, and
   a full pool waits `pthread_cond_timedwait` 5 s, returns NULL, and
   was silent. 400 ecopy threads on 16 conns = the wait. Worse:
   `put_fragments` (write.c) and `fetch_fragment` (read.c) called
   `efs_client_node_note_fail()` on that NULL, and four of them mark a
   live node DOWN for 30 s (`EFS_NODE_DOWN_FAILS`/`EFS_NODE_DOWN_MS`)
   — every RPC to it returns NULL at once until the cooldown expires.
   That is the shape of the two 28 s client-wide freezes in the ecopy
   strace (01:11:06–:34, 01:11:36–01:12:04: close, utimensat,
   newfstatat, pwrite all parked, then all released together) and of
   the 12 `put_fragments ... no quorum (efs_rc=-8)` lines against four
   live servers. FIX: pool timeouts are logged (rate-limited, with the
   pool size) and no longer counted as node failures; default pool 16
   → 64 per node (server cap is 4096 conns, 13 clients × 64 = 832;
   one RDMA conn pins ≈ 2.6 MB per end, slots connect lazily).
   `EFS_CLIENT_CONNS_PER_NODE` still overrides.

3. ISSUE (follows from 2): REPORT is one at a time (`report_mu`) and
