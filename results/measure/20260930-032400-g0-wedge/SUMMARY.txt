Cause (src/raft/raft.c)
  1. attach_group arms send_idx = durable_idx = replayed last_i (437637 on
     fcstor005). become_leader appends the no-op with append_local and
     never advances send_idx (only efs_raft_propose / efs_raft_submit do).
     send_ae caps every batch at send_idx, so the no-op at 437638 is
     never shipped: every AppendEntries to both followers is empty with
     prev_index 437637 and leader_commit 437638; the follower caps its
     commit at its own last_i 437637.
  2. on_ae_req answers success with match_index = the follower's last_i,
     not prev_index + nentries. fcstor004 had a stale 437638 (term 34,
     never committed) and answered the empty heartbeat with match 437638.
     The leader took it (on_ae_rep: match_index[1] = 437638), and when
     durable_idx reached 437638 (23:16Z, the group-2 UTIMENS released the
     fsync hold and host_durable_synced covered g0) try_commit found
     {self, fcstor004} for an entry fcstor004 does not hold. That is a
     false commit. The next heartbeat (prev 437638 term 41) made
     fcstor004 truncate its stale entry (TRUNC record) and reject; the
     leader lowered next_index to 437638 but match_index is never
     lowered, so the following success reply set next_index back to
     437639: a reject/accept ping-pong on every heartbeat since.
  Every ReadIndex from a follower now waits for 437638 and times out
  after 400 ms -> BUSY -> client EBUSY/ENOENT. The leader's own reads
  are fine (wait_timeouts=0). A group-0 write would unwedge it (submit
  advances send_idx past the no-op, both entries ship) but the false
  commit stays possible on every term change.

Fix (standard Raft, no design decision)
  a. on_ae_req success reply: m.match_index = in->prev_index +
     in->nentries. Rejections keep last_i as the next_index hint.
  b. become_leader / maybe_append_cold / efs_raft_change: after
     append_local, if (ae_capped && last_i > send_idx) send_idx = last_i,
     as efs_raft_propose does.
  Regression: tests/test_raft.c case with a follower holding a stale
  uncommitted tail at the new leader's no-op index; the leader must ship
  the no-op and must not commit it on that follower's reply.

Not the cause: the user's client perf (4141 samples, efs_rdma_recv_wait
4.3 %, forget_ino_on_tab 3.2 % from the 1M-entry ecrawl), fsync, the
compactor, RDMA link (rtt_avg 30 us, drop=0 on every lane).
