1. Pass 1 (no clients) — export nil, deletes never run

`cluster.sh stop --clients` then `--all` with `EFS_GC_DBG=1`. Leaders
printed every ~1.1 s:

    gc frag pass group=0 anchor=1 … records=256 ex=(nil)
    gc-pass … fkeys=438533 femit=257 ftomb=438276

`host_gc_export` is the first RAM export (`exports[0]`). That slot is
created on PUT_CHUNK / GET_CHUNK (`handler.c`), not at mkfs and not on
HELLO. With no client data RPC, `ex` is NULL, the pass breaks before
`host_gc_record`, there are no `gc del` lines and no ACK. The same
head-of-prefix 256 live records are emitted every pass. This is a
real mechanism for "identical counts" after a restart until a PUT or
GET has hit that process. It is not the live-cluster shape that prints
`gc-frag` (that line is gated on `recs > 0`, which requires an export).

4. Verdict

Closes as **not a stuck-delete set** on the live path: a record seen
with a non-OK delete and no ACK did not occur in this window. The
morning `records=126` / `femit=257` lines are the batch size, not
evidence those identities failed.

Two follow-ons, neither named as "the 126 are stuck":

- **D26 (already decided):** the pass still walks ~438 k tombstones
  (`ftomb`) to emit 256 live keys when it starts at the prefix head.
  Watermark (skip a truly empty pass) plus a `scan_from` cursor
  (resume past the last emitted key) are the pair. This investigation
  says the live records *do* drain, so a watermark of remaining live
  records will eventually reach 0; the cursor is what stops the
  tombstone rewalk while they remain.
- **Export-nil skip:** after restart, GC cannot delete until that
  process has seen a PUT or GET. Not a W50 "stuck record" and not a
  design ask — the comment in `host_gc_frag_pass` already calls this
  pre-mkfs; it is also post-restart. Bring it with D26 if the cursor
  is implemented, do not treat it as the morning 11/s GC_ACK cause
  (those `gc-frag` lines required an export).
