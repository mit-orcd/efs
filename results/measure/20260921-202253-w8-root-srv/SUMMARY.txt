w8_root_srv — server-side strace during 12 root mkdir+rmdir pairs (Sep 21 16:23)

Client (fcstor007) talks to 172.16.223.57 (fcstor003, g0 leader) and .58
(fcstor004, dual host). Under strace 10 of 24 ops were slow (0.74–1.44 s).

What the four efsd showed inside every slow op:
- fcstor004 (dual host, follower of both groups): 3–5 threads in one futex
  wait that all release at the SAME instant, 0.70 s / 1.05 s after they
  started — waiters on h->mu / applied_cv released when the pump finished
  one apply.
- fcstor005: one futex wait ETIMEDOUT at exactly 0.400 s = the
  host_wait_applied / host_read_index deadline (HOST_READ_TRIES x 5 ms)
  -> EFS_ERR_BUSY to the caller. This is the BUSY the client retries on.
- fcstor003 / fcstor006 (leaders): peer-conn recvfrom gaps of 0.7–1.1 s —
  no raft/peer traffic reached those conns while the apply was stalled.

Recurring quanta: 0.40 s (deadline), ~0.70 s, ~1.05 s (=0.7+0.4 or two
stalls). Nothing in the syscall list is disk: no fdatasync/pwrite >= 0.15 s.
The stall is on-CPU inside the apply — see 20260921-202715-w8-root-perf.

Consequence chain: RESOLVE apply on the root's shard takes 0.7–1.1 s under
h->mu -> host_wait_applied hits its 400 ms deadline -> BUSY -> client
backoff/retry (up to 16 attempts = 10.4 s) -> a retried UNLINK whose first
attempt had committed returns NOT_FOUND -> the "rmdir ENOENT on a dir I just
made" seen in 20260921-194332-w8-root.
