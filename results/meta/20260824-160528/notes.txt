Baseline before op-path fixes. bits=3 efs-test, fcstor007, 5000 files / 64 dirs.

Bench-self profile (5000 files, 4 workers, --perf): dominant symbol
efs_rdma_recv_wait 37%. No snprintf/malloc/strtok in the timed path.
Bench-owned userspace is noise; the bench waits on the server.

Trust w=1 as the single-stream baseline (matches ecopy's iodepth=1):
  create   604 ops/s
  stat     355 ops/s   (full-path walk; the hot path)
  getattr 3793 ops/s   (by ino)
  setattr 1206 ops/s   (2 RPCs/file)
  rename  1790 ops/s
  unlink   365 ops/s
  readdir  ~1.2M ops/s (tiny dirs; not a limiter)

w=4/16: writes scale poorly (create 604 -> 1222 -> 1523) as expected
from g_server->lock. Some read phases look too fast or getattr-flaky
(w=4 getattr 3750 fail once); do not use those as the A/B number.

Target after stages 1-2: stat/setattr >= 5-10x vs w=1, create/unlink >= 2-3x.
