Round 9 (hash every mkdir, not only dests under the export root)

Independent directories must not share one shard owner. r6 hashed only
ROOT children, so ecopy_rounds dests under /ecopy/<host>/ still piled
onto one node. Nested dirs now hash(parent, name); the inode lives on
that shard, the dentry stays on the parent (readdir). LOOKUP GETATTR
stitches a cross-shard dir stub (the r1 hardlink/futimens miss).

Fair 60s ecopy dest=r6fresh, FUSE_OK, ERR=0 (no futimens ENOENT):
  creates 1778+3428+3995+3514 = 12715 ≈ 212/s across all 4 servers.
  create_shard=51 (mkdir hops). setattr~1/create. getattr=0. gens 36×4.

1-client is slower than r8's single-shard 3850/s (every nested mkdir
leaves the dest owner). 9 clients with different dest names now land
on different nodes — that is the PFS shape.
