Round 6 (hash directories created under ROOT)

An ecopy dest is mkdir'd under the export root. Its inode now lives on
hash(1, destname), so file creates go to that shard's owner — not the
metadata-flush primary. Nested dirs stay on the dest shard (same-node
rename/hardlink). Full-tree dir hashing was reverted in round 1.

Fresh mkfs 60s ecopy dest=r6fresh:
  FILES=6754 DIRS=79 ≈ 112 files/s. ERR=0. 0 ino_dup. gens 11×4
  (extras commit; main gen barely moves).

RPC-PROF:
  fcstor003 (primary): create=1 lookup=5 getattr=0
  fcstor004 (dest owner): create=6960 lookup=8238 getattr=13508 report=6058

The "primary" is no longer on the create path. Same-dir creates still
serialize on that directory's shard lock (~112 files/s on one node).
